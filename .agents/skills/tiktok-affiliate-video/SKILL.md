## Inputs and handoffs

1. Collect the product's exact listing or confirmed facts, **one or more clear product photos**, and **one background photo** (empty, or with explicitly identified objects to remove in the compositing stage). Obtain references required to verify product geometry before final prompts; do not ask again for a usable background already supplied. A shop logo is a separate asset. Treat seller claims and example videos as unverified until checked against the chosen listing and photos. Before scripting, research current platform guidance and recent creative patterns for the product category and sales market. Prefer platform-primary sources and current trend/search tools; save the date, direct links, and the creative decision each source supports. Do not label a style “trending” based on intuition alone. See [the trend-led affiliate playbook](../../../workflow/TREND-LED-AFFILIATE-PLAYBOOK.md). Choose the relevant category skill and **one source template for the entire video** from [the template registry](../../../workflow/PROMPT-MODES.md); record its path and SHA-256 in the video's `video-profile.json`. If the document offers multiple plausible templates and the intended format is unclear, ask the user to choose before generation.
2. Write a whole-video 9:16 script first: viewer situation/question, first-1–3-second visual hook, total duration, scene order, time range, filmable action, camera/sound direction, narration, on-screen text, and factual basis. Match the format to the product/category and give viewers a visible demonstration or useful proof. Each Flow prompt is for **one scene of up to about 10 seconds**, never the entire script. Incorporate user revisions before generating; ask about the script only when a missing choice materially changes the video.
3. For **every Flow scene of this video**, write a scene-specific prefix naming the exact QA-passed storyboard file/version/hash, one planned action, camera view, continuity, and forbidden inventions. Keep original product/background files as provenance, not raw Flow compositing inputs. Append the **same locked template** unchanged using `scripts/compose_flow_prompt.py --profile <video-profile.json>`. Use --override for the explicit storyboard/no-speech precedence block after the unchanged template; inspect the full composed prompt. Scene-specific prefix and override may vary while the source template stays fixed. If a generated video scene fails review, replace it with a newly generated video or another reviewed video clip; never insert a still image as a substitute. If no reviewed video replacement is available, leave the scene unresolved and report the blocker. Do not swap to a second template mid-video. Label the prefix as newly written, not copied from the user's document.
4. Inspect the current Flow UI for supported image-to-video mode, model, duration, resolution and price; historical screenshots are not defaults. Use the approved composite as start frame, optional reviewed end state only when justified. Submit one representative scene and QA it before continuing the technique. When this user has given standing permission for in-scope Flow generations on their Pro account, do not ask again about routine generation credits. Keep generations within this project's scope. This does not authorize purchases, plan changes, unrelated generations, or unrelated paid services. If no standing permission applies, follow the user's explicit authorization before spending credits.
5. `affiliate_visual_qa` reviews every generated clip against the photos before editing. Watch the whole clip and inspect a contact sheet for every source frame (usually 24 fps); a 2 fps sheet is only an initial scan. Count visible hands and objects in every frame; any brief extra/duplicated hand, merged geometry, material/product mismatch, or impossible contact fails the clip. Record exact file, full-frame coverage, timestamps, evidence, and pass/fix/reject in a review sheet. Fix/reject a failed scene and review the video replacement. Never treat a sparse beginning/middle/end sample as sufficient; keep the original fixed source template unchanged.
6. `affiliate_post` assembles only accepted video clips in CapCut according to the current script. Never use a still image to replace a failed video scene. Use reviewed ElevenLabs V3 Adam narration at the user-selected 1.2x, applied once. Keep exact raw TTS input separate from clean captions; align captions to the final-speed track. Flow clips provide visuals/optional ambience only; remove any accidentally generated dialogue. Follow [voice policy](../../../workflow/VOICE-POLICY.md). For a Gemini/Flow watermark, use the exact supplied shop image as a circular mask centered on the measured symbol; choose its size by checking both brand readability on a phone and clearance from the product/controls, then record asset, diameter, and placement in project configuration. Do not invent replacement branding or cover the full corner. Align captions and basic branding with the narration, then hand off the baseline draft and approved assets.
7. `pro_video_editor` refines story pacing, continuity, captions, sound and transitions. Choose each edit point for meaning and visual/audio continuity: clean cut on action, match cut in shape/position, or sound bridge first; use a short dissolve/fade only for a real location/time change when it does not ghost the product, hands, or text. Do not substitute a more noticeable effect when the underlying shots are weak; reselect or recut the shots. Keep narration and title timing aligned. For managed CapCut drafts, use the installed post-editing workflow and supported transition atomic functions; do not claim a cloud edit if the draft cannot be updated. Never add static frames to cover defective footage. See [editorial standards](../../../workflow/EDITORIAL-STANDARDS.md).
8. `affiliate_visual_qa` is the final video release gate: review the complete saved/exported video at normal speed, inspect every transition frame by frame, verify the voice source and Vietnamese script, check captions, product identity, absence of stills, TikTok-safe text placement, and audio quality. Record the exact output, draft version, and coverage; do not report completion while a final defect remains.
9. After that exact export passes, `affiliate_publishing` prepares the TikTok post package. Research recent Vietnam/category examples and platform guidance on the day of export, inspect the approved MP4 and verified listing, then write one copy-ready caption plus a distinct alternative, relevant hashtags, rationale, source URLs/date, and claim checks to `publishing/post-package.md`. Re-run for a revised export. Never label unverified hashtags as trending or promise viral reach; do not post automatically. See [caption and hashtag playbook](../../../workflow/POST-CAPTION-HASHTAG-PLAYBOOK.md).
